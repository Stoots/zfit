# Shared Fitness Vocabulary

Terms shared across the web app, server, and mobile app for training and performance tracking.

## Training and testing

**Training Program**:
A multi-week structure for working toward fitness goals, organized into phases and weeks with planned sessions.
_Avoid_: Workout plan (when referring to the whole multi-week structure)

**Workout Plan**:
A set of scheduled workout sessions with exercises or presets; it can be used within a training program or independently.
_Avoid_: Training program (when referring to a set of sessions rather than the multi-week structure)

**Workout Session**:
A single workout within a Workout Plan, with its own ordered exercises and prescription. Sessions with the same label, such as A or B, may have different prescriptions in different weeks.
_Avoid_: Workout plan (when referring to one workout within a plan)

**Session Prescription**:
The intended exercises, sets, repetitions, effort, rest and other targets for a particular Workout Session, distinct from the results recorded when it is performed.

**Optional Session**:
A session offered in addition to the required training, which the athlete may omit without failing to complete the required schedule.

**Omitted Session**:
An Optional Session not performed on its scheduled day, distinct from a missed required session.

**Cardio Test**:
A repeatable fitness assessment performed under a defined protocol, with each attempt recording measured results.
_Avoid_: Cardio workout (when referring to an assessment intended to compare performance over time)

**Test Attempt**:
One dated performance of a cardio test, with the measurements and notes recorded for that performance.
_Avoid_: Test result (when referring to the complete dated record rather than one measured value)

**Program Phase**:
A segment of a training program that groups weeks of training around a goal.

**Planned Session**:
A workout or cardio test scheduled within a training program before it occurs.

**Session Outcome**:
The recorded result of a Planned Session: completed, missed or omitted. A Planned Session without a Session Outcome is pending. A workout's Session Outcome is final; a Cardio Test's lasts only while a Test Attempt is linked to it.
_Avoid_: Status (when `planned` is meant to be a result)

**Completed Session**:
A Planned Session performed as planned, through a linked diary workout or a linked Test Attempt.

**Missed Session**:
A planned session explicitly recorded as missed. It remains recorded as missed even when the program's auto-shift policy moves future sessions.
