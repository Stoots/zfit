## Saving a food entry on web

Choose the quantity and unit, optionally set the time and entry note, then select **Add to Meal**. The form closes after the entry is saved. While saving, both Add buttons and the form controls are disabled to prevent duplicate entries.

If saving fails, the form keeps your quantity, selected unit, time and note and shows an error. Select **Add to Meal** again to retry, or **Cancel** to discard the draft.
