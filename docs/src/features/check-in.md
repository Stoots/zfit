# Check-In

The Measurements tab puts the daily check-in form before the weight trend. On a phone, Weight and Save Check-In are available without scrolling. Choose the date above the tabs, enter your weight in your preferred units, and save. Previous measurements appear as hints; use **Use last** to record a suggested value for the selected day.

The complete measurement form remains available below Weight, including body measurements, steps, body composition and custom categories. The trend and recent activity follow the form and refresh after a successful save. If saving fails, your entered values remain available for retry.
