# A Cardio Test's Session Outcome follows its Test Attempt; a workout's is final

A Planned Session bound to a Cardio Test counts as completed only while a Test
Attempt is linked to it. Moving or unlinking the attempt makes the session
pending again: it is not marked missed, auto-shift does not run, and earlier
shifts are not undone. A workout's Session Outcome is recorded once and cannot be
undone. The asymmetry is deliberate. A Test Attempt is an independent record the
athlete may reassign, so the outcome is derived from the link. A completed
workout is diary history created through the Program, and undoing it would mean
deciding what to do with the logged sets, which ADR 0003's history-preservation
rules forbid. Do not "make them consistent" without revisiting both reasons.
