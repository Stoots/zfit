# Model multiweek training programs separately from workout plans

The existing workout plan represents a dated weekly or sequential schedule of exercise sessions, but it has no program phases or goals. Add a distinct multiweek Training Program organized by goal-bearing phases and weeks; planned workout sessions can reuse existing workout plans, completed workouts link to planned sessions, and cardio tests may be scheduled in a phase or recorded independently. Keep fixed dates as the default, offer an auto-shift policy for future sessions, and retain missed sessions in the history.
